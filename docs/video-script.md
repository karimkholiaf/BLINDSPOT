# Demo video — script and shot list

**Hard limit: 2:00.** Over that and judges don't watch it at all. Target **1:50** so the upload has margin.

Read at a natural pace, not rushed. The word counts below assume ~150 wpm. Pauses are marked and matter — the silence while the diagnosis appears is doing work.

---

## The structure

Most hackathon demos open by explaining the product. This one opens by making the judge fail, then explains. The blind-spot test costs 12 seconds and buys the rest of the video, because after it the value proposition needs no argument.

| # | Time | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|------|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | 0:00–0:14 | Hero. Cursor drifts to the ✛ and dot. Hold still. | "Close your left eye and look at the cross. Lean in. — The dot vanished, and you didn't see a hole. Your brain filled the gap with more background, confidently, without telling you." |

## The spoken wrong explanation (shot 5)

Deliver this **confidently**, at normal pace, like someone who is sure. That's the whole point — it has to sound right.

> "So Big-O measures how fast an algorithm runs. You work out the complexity and that tells you the running time. Quicksort is O(n log n), insertion sort is O(n squared), so quicksort is always going to be the faster algorithm — that's why every real sorting library uses quicksort."

Roughly 12 seconds spoken. Do not stumble on it; re-record until it sounds natural, because a hesitant delivery ruins the reveal.

## Recording notes

- **Window at 1280×800.** Larger and the text is unreadable when Devpost scales the video down.
- **Hide bookmarks bar and any personal tabs.** A judge sees everything in frame.
- **Cursor movement is slow and deliberate.** Fast erratic cursor reads as nervous.
- **Do not narrate the UI** ("now I'm clicking the button"). The screen already shows that. Narrate meaning.
- **Shot 6 is the most important pause in the video.** Two full seconds of the loading line with no voiceover. It builds the expectation that the app is about to agree with you.

## Captions

Burn in captions for the whole video regardless of voiceover — many judges watch muted on a first pass. Keep them bottom-centre, two lines maximum, high contrast.
